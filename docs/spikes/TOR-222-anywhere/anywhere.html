Growing tomatoes in a cold summer

Nothing on this page has anything to do with torrents.

Not enough accumulated warmth. Ripening needs a run of days above about 20 °C, and a cold summer never delivers it - the fruit sets and then simply waits.

Pick them and ripen them indoors with a banana in the bag. The ethylene does the rest in about a week.

Ethylene switches on the genes for lycopene synthesis and for the cell-wall enzymes that soften the flesh. A banana is simply the cheapest source of it in a kitchen.
