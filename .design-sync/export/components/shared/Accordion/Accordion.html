Level 1 again - the same box with nothing around it

Not a seventh state: the same open level-1 disclosure as two panes up, with .run-table-wrap and .run-grid removed. This pane is the decoupling rendered rather than asserted, and it is measurable off this card - the two rows resolve BYTE-IDENTICAL tracks (320px 104px 140px 54.3984px ×4 86.3984px 54.3984px 69.6094px) and identical cell offsets to the last decimal, because a row reads --run-tracks from :root and is its own grid (TOR-221). What it loses is the TABLE's, not the disclosure's: the wrap's ground, its horizontal scroll, and its type size - the row here is 14px where the wrapped one is 13.12px, since the .82rem comes from the wrap. So a disclosure needs no particular container to lay out; it inherits type from wherever it is put, like any element.
